Designed with reverence for sound.

Every element in Mumixic is stripped of bloat and tuned for tactile responsiveness, aesthetic poise, and musical fidelity.

Living Mesh Gradients

Fluid multi-stop mesh gradients breathe with the album artwork. Smooth, continuous color adaptation transforms your screen into an immersive canvas without jarring cuts.

Adjustability: 10% – 100% background visibility slider with integrated black scrim for high contrast legibility.

Synchronized Lyrics

Read and sing along with millisecond-accurate time-synced lyrics. Smooth auto-scrolling keeps the active line highlighted while unfocused verses gently fade.

Capabilities: Multi-provider lyric fetching, Romanization, translation, and interactive tap-to-seek.

Lossless & High Bitrate

Dual-source streaming powered by YouTube Music and JioSaavn high-fidelity audio streams. Enjoy transparent playback without compression artifacts or intrusive interruptions.

Audio Engine: Precision ExoPlayer sink, sample-accurate crossfade, and automatic stream quality upgrade.

Curated Typography

Choose the typographic personality that speaks to you. Seamlessly toggle between SF Pro, Classical Serif, clean Sans-Serif, Monospace, or Cursive directly inside Settings.

Built-in: Live in-app preview with zero horizontal distortion or unnatural geometric stretching.

Smart Audio Cache

Smart LRU disk caching ensures your recently played and favorite tracks are instant-ready and fully playable offline without re-consuming cellular bandwidth.

Control: Configurable cache quotas, read-ahead chunk buffering, and instant one-tap cache purge.

Multi-Platform Ecosystem

Engineered with Kotlin Multiplatform. Experience Mumixic seamlessly on Android, Wear OS smartwatches, Windows 10/11 desktops, and macOS workstations.

Continuity: OTA in-app update checks, Discord Rich Presence, and media notification controls.

Technical Architecture

Built modern from the ground up on Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform, and precision audio pipelines.
